## 4. Meaning and limits of approval

1. APPROVED means LAEV has approved the work-package scope and planned deliverables.
2. APPROVED does not mean COMPLETED, ACCEPTED, VERIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, or independently reviewed.
3. Execution must follow the dependency matrix in WORKPLAN-PHASE-0.
4. A work package whose dependencies are not satisfied remains approved in scope but cannot begin gated execution.
5. Approval of WP-0.12 does not close Phase 0, approve its compliance report, or authorize Phase 1.
6. No production, institutional operation, or live 911 deployment is authorized by this record.
7. The status of every deliverable and residual limitation remains evidence-based and must not be upgraded merely because the work-package scope is approved.

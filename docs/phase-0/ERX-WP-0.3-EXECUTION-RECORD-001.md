## 5. Candidate hierarchy for LAEV consideration — NOT ADOPTED

The available evidence supports distinguishing roles, but does not yet justify an unconditional precedence rule. Candidate arrangement:

1. **LAEV-approved governance and decision records** — control project authorization, approval, and accepted dispositions within their stated scope; do not rewrite technical source content unless explicitly approved as an amendment.
2. **Foundational technical sources** — Protocol, Atlas, UTXO-911, Roadmap, and Technical Response, each retained with its own scope and provenance; no global rank among them is adopted in this record.
3. **Approved derivative specifications and decisions** — may clarify or amend a source only when explicit approval, scope, affected versions, and traceability are recorded.
4. **Working drafts, registers, analysis, and implementation artifacts** — support review and traceability; they are not normative merely because they are in the repository.

This candidate hierarchy is an analytical proposal. LAEV must determine whether to adopt, revise, or reject it. Missing cited sources are not assigned an invented rank or content.
